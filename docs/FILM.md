# The film's vault heist, beat by beat, and what the game makes of it

Notes from watching the film's last act end to end (a 22-minute clip, frame by frame; the
clip itself is reference only and never goes in this repo). Part one says what happens on
screen, in our own words. Part two sets each beat against the game: covered, missing, or
there but thin. Part three turns the gaps into game design. All dialogue in the game stays
original, and so do any people we draw.

## Part one: what happens

**1. The setup.** Aerials over Rio, then police headquarters: flags, armed guards at the door.
The villain, Reyes, and his enforcer are walked inside, and the corrupt commander shows them
the evidence vault through a glass wall. It sits in a strong room on the ground floor.

**2. The break-in.** Hobbs drives a grey armored truck up out of an underpass, with Elena
beside him. The truck smashes through the striped boom gate with its stop sign at the
entrance. Cops on the ledges and ramps open fire, bullets sparking off the armor. The two
black Chargers drift round the corner behind it. The truck dives into the station's
underground garage, which is lit cold blue with rows of fluorescent tubes, pillars and parked
cruisers. It bulldozes a cruiser and rams the garage wall straight into the strong room. The
blocks blow inward and the truck backs out with rubble on its roof.

**3. The hook-up under fire.** The Chargers swing round and reverse up to the hole. Each has a
steel cage on its tail around a winch drum. Hobbs and Elena get out and hold the cops off with
rifles and pistols while Dom and Brian run the cable hooks through the hole by hand and latch
them onto the vault's handles. Upstairs, the commander and Reyes realise what is happening,
and the cops pour down the stairwell.

**4. The pull.** The cars sit side by side and do burnouts, filling the garage with tyre smoke.
The cables go bar-tight through the hole. The vault rocks, then tears out of the wall in a
shower of concrete, flattening the commander as it goes. It bursts out and the cars launch.

**5. The breakout.** The vault tumbles through the garage while cops dive out of its way.
Cruisers burn out to give chase. The convoy erupts up the exit ramp into daylight. The vault
swings through a line of bollards, smashes the corner of a stone wall into gravel, and keeps
coming while cops fire from a balcony.

**6. The city chase.** This is the longest stretch and almost all of it is about the vault as
a weapon.
- Several police forces join in: white civil-police cruisers, blue-and-white patrol cars,
  yellow-and-blue highway SUVs, special-ops trucks.
- Mia guides the convoy by radio from a laptop map in a dark hideout.
- Pedestrians scatter at busy crossings. Kids watch from a passing bus. Top-down shots show
  the vault snaking on its two cables.
- A cop throws a spike strip. A roadblock goes up at an intersection. A drift through a
  corner swings the vault wide into a cruiser.
- The vault goes through the glass front of a bank, crushes a cruiser and sideswipes
  parked cars.
- From inside a police car we see the vault swing straight at the camera.
- Motorcycle cops in white helmets weave between building columns. The vault sweeps them
  away, and one rider is flung over a windshield.
- Street furniture goes: café umbrellas, a bus shelter, lamp posts. Cars flip and
  cartwheel, and sparks pour off the vault's base.
- Cops lean out of their windows firing pistols. A cruiser ends up in a tree, and parked
  cars and a kiosk are ploughed flat.
- Han and Roman, in police uniforms and police cars, drive inside the pack and wreck real
  cruisers from within. One SUV flips and a taxi is caught. Roman is laughing on the radio.

**7. The bridge.** The convoy slides through an intersection, under an overpass and out onto a
long bridge over the bay, with Sugarloaf behind. Reyes and his men join in black SUVs and a
pickup, grinning, with a huge pack of police behind them. On the radio, Dom decides there are
too many and fires a remote release: the latch on Brian's cable pops in a spray of sparks and
Brian peels away, furious. Dom, alone, throws the car through a 180 so it faces the pack
nose-on. He does a burnout and flips three nitrous switches, each with a green light. A
pressure gauge reads, he presses the button, and the car launches with its front wheels in
the air, dragging the stopped vault.

**8. The wrecking ball.** Dom drives head-on into the pack and swings the vault like a flail.
- A cruiser has its windshield smashed in, another is crushed against the barrier, and
  others are flipped onto their sides.
- A pickup and a cruiser go over the rail into the bay in huge splashes.
- Reyes's gunmen stand up through the SUV sunroofs with heavy guns, and the Charger is
  riddled with holes.
- Dom swings the vault over the median into Reyes's SUV. The vault stops dead, the Charger is
  yanked into the air and comes down crushed on top of the SUV, and Dom crawls out by the
  vault at the bridge's edge.

**9. The showdown.** Reyes's enforcer aims at Dom, but Brian has come back on foot and gets to
him first. Hobbs's truck arrives. Hobbs takes Reyes. There is a standoff across the vault, and
Hobbs gives Dom and Brian a day's head start before he comes after them. They drive off
together.

**10. The reveal.** Hobbs opens the vault: it is empty. Then we see what happened earlier. Under
an overpass just before the bridge, a garbage truck with its rear hopper raised and a ramp
down swallowed the real vault as the convoy straddled it. The decoy, waiting under a tarp,
was clipped onto the cables. A police SUV drove straight past the truck, its back now hidden
under the tarp, and saw nothing. Hobbs, standing inside the empty vault, starts to laugh.

**11. The epilogue.** In the hideout, the real vault is cracked with a fingerprint and a drill,
and the money pours out. The crew celebrates, sends a share to a family who helped them, and
scatters around the world.

## Part three: game design

The shape stays the same as in MOMENTS.md: telegraph, action window, payoff, consequence.
The rule is that every film beat becomes something the player does, then a cinematic
payoff. The film's own shot choices (low cameras, long lenses, slow motion at the moment of
impact) are the language for the payoffs.

### The rolling swap (replaces the stop-and-swap)
- **Telegraph.** Mia calls the tunnel. Inside, a city garbage truck (our own livery) is rolling
  ahead with its hopper raised and the ramp down, sparks off the ramp's lip. Gisele drives;
  Tej rides the back step waving them in. Han drops the gate behind the convoy so no cop sees.
- **Action.** Thread the needle: line the vault up with the ramp, matching the truck's speed.
  A guide on the road shows the line, and a LINE-UP meter reads how centred and how matched
  you are. The partner takes the far side of the truck.
- **Payoff.** Slow motion as the cars straddle the truck and the vault rides up the ramp into
  the hopper. The cables pop off in sparks and the hopper slams down like a jaw. Tej flicks
  the hooks onto the decoy waiting ahead under a tarp. The cables snap tight, the decoy jerks
  into motion and the tarp flies off it. The convoy bursts out into the daylight with the decoy.
- **Consequence.** A clean line-up earns a bonus. A sloppy one still goes in, because there is
  no fail here. Behind them, the cops come through the gate and drive straight past the
  parked garbage truck while Tej leans on it and waves. Keep that on screen for a beat.

### The wrecking ball (the bridge climax)
- After the solo pull, the wall of cruisers at the far end **starts its engines and comes at
  you**, with Reyes's two black SUVs behind it.
- **The flail.** On one cable, steering hard plus the handbrake whips the vault out across the
  lanes. A cruiser it hits in the finale is thrown much harder. Hit near the rail, it goes
  **over the side into the bay** in slow motion with a splash, and that is the news headline.
- **Gunners.** Reyes's men stand up through the sunroofs and fire. You see muzzle flashes,
  tracers and sparks off your car, and hear the rounds. There is no health bar. It is
  pressure, and the reason to finish him.
- **The last swing.** Swing the vault into Reyes's SUV: slow motion, the SUV folds and rolls,
  and your car is yanked into the air and lands on its roof beside it. There is no fail; if it
  drags on, Reyes rams you and the swing comes round anyway.

### The ending (replaces the end-screen text)
- **Showdown.** Hobbs's armored truck arrives with the cruisers behind it. He takes Reyes and
  gives the crew a day's head start, and the partner pulls up to collect the player.
- **The reveal.** Hobbs swings the decoy's door open: empty. Mia on the radio has the last word.
- **Payday.** In the hideout, the real vault's door swings open and the money pours out, then
  the results.

### The breakout and the city
- **Boom gate.** The truck snaps the striped barrier at the entrance; the arm spins away.
- **Covering fire.** Muzzle flashes from beside the truck while the cables go on.
- **Bollards and stone wall.** The street outside the station has a row of bollards and a
  stone planter on the vault's line out; the first swing scatters them.
- **Cops shoot from windows.** Cruisers close behind fire pistols from the driver's window,
  with flashes, sparks off the vault and the cars, and pings. It is pressure only.
- **Flips and cartwheels.** Cruisers hit by the vault at speed roll over instead of only
  spinning.
- **Han and Roman.** Two cruisers that aren't what they seem join the pack behind you. They
  ram real cops off the road for half a minute, laughing on the radio, then peel off: "see
  you at the tunnel".
- **Bike cops** (next): fast, fragile riders that weave through traffic; the vault sweeps them.

### The split and the solo pull
- **The latch.** A close shot of the partner's cable latch popping in a spray of sparks.
- **Switches and wheelie.** The first press of Shift in the solo pull flips three switches on
  the dash, one at a time with a green light and a click, and lights the button. When the
  vault lurches free, the nose lifts in a wheelie.
